Chapainawabganj District: Mango, Agriculture & Economy

1. Introduction: The "Mango Capital of Bangladesh"
Chapainawabganj is universally recognized as the "Mango Capital of Bangladesh" (আমের রাজধানী) due to its unmatched volume, variety, and historical heritage of mango cultivation. The agro-climatic conditions, characterized by well-drained fertile soil and optimal thermal variations, provide an ideal ecosystem for cultivating world-class mangoes.

Key Premium Mango Varieties:
- Khirsapat (Himsagar): Celebrated for its rich sweetness, smooth texture, and deep aromatic flavor, holding a prestigious GI (Geographical Indication) tag.
- Langra: One of the most sought-after varieties, known for its distinct citrusy aroma, fiberless pulp, and delightful tangy-sweet taste.
- Fazli: Famous for its massive size, late-season harvest, thick pulp, and excellent shelf life.
- Ashwina / Ashwini: A very late-ripening variety that extends the mango season deep into the monsoon, supporting prolonged trade.
- Amrapali & Others: Popular hybrid and local commercial varieties grown extensively across orchards (bagans) throughout the upazilas.

2. Key Economic Activities & The Kansat Mango Market
The district's economy is predominantly agrarian, deeply anchored in horticulture, seasonal agricultural trade, and small-scale manufacturing.

- Agricultural Backbone: Apart from mangoes, the region produces large quantities of winter vegetables, pulses (lentils, chickpea), mustard, and paddy (rice), particularly utilizing the fertile alluvial plains and the Barind tract.
- Kansat Mango Market (কানসাট আমের বাজার): 
  - Located in Shibganj Upazila, Kansat hosts the largest wholesale mango market in South Asia (or at least the country).
  - During peak season (May to August), thousands of traders, orchard owners, truck drivers, and seasonal laborers converge here daily.
  - Billions of taka in transactions occur, driving local commerce, transportation, cold-chain logistics, and packing industries.

3. Cottage Industries and the Silk Industry (রেশম বা সিল্ক শিল্প)
Beyond agriculture, Chapainawabganj boasts a rich tradition of cottage and handicraft industries.

- The Silk Capital (সিল্ক সিটি): 
  - Historically and culturally, Chapainawabganj—along with neighboring Rajshahi—is synonymous with the Bangladeshi silk industry.
  - Sericulture: Mulberry cultivation and silkworm rearing (cocoon production) are practiced by local farming communities, particularly involving women artisans in rural pockets.
  - Reeling and Weaving: Raw silk is processed, spun, and woven into exquisite pure silk fabrics, sarees, and garments.
- Other Cottage Crafts: 
  - Traditional pottery, cane and bamboo work, and brass-metal crafts also contribute to the traditional artisanal economy of the district.
