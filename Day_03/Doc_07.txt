Biotechnology and Genomics
CRISPR Gene Editing Technology
Biotechnology merges biological sciences with engineering principles to develop innovative medical and agricultural solutions. The discovery of CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing technology has revolutionized molecular biology by allowing precise, targeted modifications of DNA sequences.

Medical Therapeutics and Diagnostics
Modern biopharmaceutical research utilizes recombinant DNA techniques and monoclonal antibodies to engineer targeted cancer treatments and advanced vaccines. Diagnostic tools powered by genomic sequencing enable early detection of hereditary disorders before clinical symptoms manifest.

Bioethics and Regulatory Oversight
The immense power to alter genetic material raises complex bioethical questions concerning germline editing and genetic privacy. International regulatory bodies maintain strict oversight frameworks to balance scientific progress with patient safety and ecological protection.
