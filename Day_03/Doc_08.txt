Quantum Computing Principles
Qubits vs. Classical Bits
Quantum computing leverages the fundamental laws of quantum mechanics to solve computational problems that are intractable for classical supercomputers. Unlike classical bits that represent binary states of zero or one, quantum bits or qubits utilize superposition.

Superposition and Entanglement
Superposition allows qubits to exist in multiple states simultaneously, exponentially increasing processing capacity. Furthermore, quantum entanglement links qubits such that the physical state of one instantly dictates the state of another, regardless of distance.

Cryptography and Future Applications
While quantum processors hold immense promise for optimizing complex financial models and molecular drug discovery, they also pose a direct threat to standard RSA encryption algorithms. Consequently, cryptographers are actively developing post-quantum cryptographic standards.
