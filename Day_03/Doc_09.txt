Marine Ecosystems and Conservation
Coral Reef Biodiversity hotspots
Marine ecosystems cover over seventy percent of the Earth's surface and harbor staggering biological diversity. Coral reefs act as vital underwater nurseries, supporting thousands of marine species while protecting coastal communities from severe storm surges.

Ocean Acidification and Warming
Rising atmospheric carbon dioxide concentrations lead directly to ocean acidification and elevated sea surface temperatures. These environmental stressors trigger widespread coral bleaching events and disrupt marine food webs on a global scale.

Conservation Strategies and Marine Parks
Protecting oceanic health requires establishing large-scale marine protected areas (MPAs), enforcing sustainable fishing quotas, and curbing plastic pollution at river estuaries. International cooperation is indispensable for preserving fragile marine habitats for future generations.
