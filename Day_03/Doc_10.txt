Neuroscience and Brain Mechanics
Neural Networks and Synapses
Neuroscience investigates the intricate structure and function of the human nervous system. The brain consists of approximately eighty-six billion neurons communicating via electrochemical signals across specialized junctions known as synapses.

Cognitive Plasticity and Memory
Neuroplasticity demonstrates the remarkable ability of the brain to reorganize neural pathways in response to learning, environmental changes, or physical trauma. Memory formation involves synaptic strengthening through long-term potentiation across hippocampal networks.

Neurological Disorders and Treatment
Advancements in functional neuroimaging, such as fMRI and EEG, allow researchers to map neural activity with high spatial resolution. This capability accelerates the development of targeted therapies for neurodegenerative conditions like Alzheimer's and Parkinson's disease.
